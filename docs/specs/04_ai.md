# 04. AI & Vision Specification

## Overview
Defines how Enemies spawn, acquire targets (Aggro), and navigate the environment.

## Spawning
- **Spawners**: Placed at fixed locations. Spawn 1 Enemy every N ticks.

## Aggro & Field of View (FOV)
An enemy will only acquire a Player as a target if ALL of the following conditions are met:
1. **Distance**: The player is within `AggroRange` (8.0 units).
2. **Angle**: The player is within a 120-degree cone originating from the Enemy's `Heading` (±60 degrees).
3. **Line of Sight (LOS)**: A 2D Raycast (Line Segment) drawn from the Enemy's position to the Player's position does NOT intersect any Wall AABB.

If a target is acquired, the Enemy sets `TargetID`, updates its `Heading` to face the player, and moves or shoots towards them based on equipped weapon range.

## Idle & Target Clearing Behavior
If an Enemy loses Line of Sight or the player exits vision/aggro range:
- `TargetID` is immediately reset to `""`.
- **Velocity**: Reset to `[0,0,0]`.
- **Looking Around**: The Enemy rotates its `Heading` by a fixed amount per tick (approximately 90 degrees per second) to sweep its FOV cone, acting as a radar.

## BDD Scenarios

### Scenario: Line of Sight Blocked
- **Given** a Wall between a Player and an Enemy.
- **When** the Player is within 8.0 units and within the Enemy's 120-degree FOV cone.
- **Then** the Raycast intersects the Wall, LOS is blocked, and the Enemy does not aggro.
- **Verification**: `vision_test.go` -> `TestVision_LineOfSight`

### Scenario: Spawner Generation
- **Given** a Spawner with interval 30
- **When** 30 ticks pass in the simulation
- **Then** exactly 1 new Enemy is added to the WorldState.
